ComplySight — Sample Requests & Test Scenarios
================================================================================
Use these pre-structured scenarios to test the ComplySight Streamlit UI or API.
Each scenario has been verified end-to-end against the governing policy rules.

Copy and paste each field directly into the Streamlit application.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 1: COMPLIANT
Policy Domain:    Password & Authentication Policy (§4.8, §6.1)
Expected Verdict: COMPLIANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employee Name:          Sarah Jenkins
Department:             Engineering
Request:                I am setting up a nightly ETL job that extracts data from the sales database. I have configured it to use a non-interactive service account. I haven't hardcoded the credentials into the Python script; instead, the script fetches the API token dynamically from our enterprise secret vault, which is configured to rotate the secret every 30 days.
Reason:                 Need automated system access without interactive login.
Additional Information: Non-interactive service account, enterprise secret vault integration with 30-day automated rotation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO 2: NON_COMPLIANT
Policy Domain:    Device Usage Policy (§4.4)
Expected Verdict: NON_COMPLIANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employee Name:          Sarah Jenkins
Department:             Marketing
Request:                I have downloaded and installed a peer-to-peer (P2P) file-sharing client on my company-issued laptop to help quickly distribute large promotional video files to our external media partners.
Reason:                 Attempting to bypass email attachment size limits for large marketing assets.
Additional Information: Installed BitTorrent client on company-issued laptop.

SCENARIO 3: PARTIALLY_COMPLIANT
Policy Domain:    Remote Work Policy (§4.1, §4.3, §4.5)
Expected Verdict: PARTIALLY_COMPLIANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employee Name:          David Kim
Department:             Engineering
Request:                I want to work remotely while taking care of my 2-year-old child during working hours. My remote work arrangement has been approved by my manager and HRBP. I have a dedicated workspace, VPN access, and a 100 Mbps internet connection.
Reason:                 Requesting remote work while acting as the primary caregiver.
Additional Information: Manager and HRBP approval secured, dedicated workspace verified.
