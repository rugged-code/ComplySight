# Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions
**Policy ID:** FIN-POL-005
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Finance Department

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Expense & Reimbursement Policy is to establish clear, testable guidelines for business-related expenses incurred by employees on behalf of NovaTech Solutions. This policy ensures fiscal responsibility, regulatory compliance, and timely reimbursement for legitimate business costs.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all NovaTech Solutions employees, contractors, board members, and authorized representatives who incur business expenses or travel on behalf of the company.

## 3. Definitions
*   **Business Expense:** A reasonable, necessary, and appropriately documented expense incurred specifically for the purpose of conducting NovaTech Solutions business.
*   **Itemized Receipt:** An original document from a vendor that explicitly details the date of the transaction, the specific items or services purchased, the cost of each item, and the total amount paid.
*   **Per Diem:** A fixed daily allowance granted to employees for meals and incidental expenses while traveling on official corporate business.
*   **Client Entertainment:** Expenses incurred for meals, events, or activities involving external clients or prospective clients with a clear, documented business purpose.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Receipt Requirement
An original, itemized receipt must be provided for every individual expense transaction that exceeds $25.00 USD. Credit card summary slips that do not itemize the purchase are unacceptable.

### 4.2 Submission Deadline
All expense reimbursement reports must be submitted through the centralized Expense Management System within 30 calendar days of the original transaction date. Expenses submitted after 30 days will be automatically rejected.

### 4.3 Approval Routing
All expense reports require explicit digital approval from the employee's direct manager. Any single expense report containing a total claim exceeding $5,000 USD requires mandatory secondary approval from the Department Director.

### 4.4 Air Travel Class Restrictions
All domestic flights and international flights with a total scheduled duration of under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Business class bookings are strictly restricted to international flights with a continuous travel duration exceeding 6 hours.

### 4.5 Accommodation Cost Limits
Nightly hotel room rates must not exceed $250.00 USD per night (excluding taxes and fees) in standard cities, and $350.00 USD per night in designated high-cost cities (as dynamically defined in the corporate travel booking portal).

### 4.6 Meal Allowances and Alcohol
Daily meal expenses for individual employee travel must not exceed a total of $75.00 USD per day. The purchase of alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable for individual employee meals.

### 4.7 Client Entertainment Pre-Approval
Client entertainment events or meals require documented pre-approval from a Department Director if the estimated cost exceeds $150.00 USD per attendee. The final expense report must list the full names, titles, and company affiliations of all attendees, along with the specific business topic discussed.

### 4.8 Mileage Reimbursement
The use of a personal vehicle for approved business travel is reimbursed at a flat rate of $0.65 per mile. Standard daily commuting miles between an employee's residence and their primary NovaTech Solutions office location are strictly non-reimbursable.

### 4.9 Corporate Credit Card Usage
Employees who have been issued a NovaTech Solutions corporate credit card must use the card for all eligible business expenses exceeding $100.00 USD. Taking cash advances using the corporate credit card is strictly prohibited.

### 4.10 Explicitly Non-Reimbursable Items
NovaTech Solutions will not reimburse expenses for personal grooming (e.g., haircuts), traffic citations, parking tickets, in-room hotel movies, or individual airline club/lounge memberships under any circumstances.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Finance Department:** Responsible for auditing expense reports, executing reimbursement payouts, and maintaining the Expense Management System.
*   **Direct Managers:** Responsible for reviewing their direct reports' expenses to verify that all claims are valid, within budget, and compliant with this policy prior to approval.
*   **Employees:** Responsible for exercising prudent judgment when incurring expenses, submitting accurate reports on time, and retaining required documentation.

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Executive Travel Accommodation
Members of the Executive Leadership Team (C-Suite) are exempt from the standard accommodation cost limits defined in Requirement 4.5. However, any nightly hotel rate exceeding $500.00 USD requires post-trip review by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

### 6.2 Remote Area Travel
Employees traveling to remote geographic locations where formal itemized receipts are technologically impossible to obtain are exempt from Requirement 4.1 for transactions up to $100.00 USD. A Missing Receipt Affidavit must be completed and signed by the employee for each undocumented transaction.

### 6.3 Medical Accommodations
Employees requiring specialized travel arrangements (e.g., upgraded seating, specific hotel requirements) due to documented medical conditions are exempt from the air travel class restrictions (Requirement 4.4) and accommodation limits (Requirement 4.5), provided the exception is pre-approved in writing by the Human Resources Department.

## 7. Violations and Enforcement
Failure to comply with the rules outlined in this Expense & Reimbursement Policy will result in the immediate denial of the reimbursement claim. Repeated violations, submission of fraudulent expenses, or intentional circumvention of approval controls will result in severe disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and civil legal action.

## 8. Policy Review
The Finance Department, in coordination with the Compliance Office, will review and update this policy comprehensively on an annual basis to ensure ongoing alignment with tax laws, market conditions, and corporate financial objectives.
