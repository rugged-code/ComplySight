# Remote Work Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions
**Policy ID:** HR-POL-030
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Human Resources Department

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Remote Work Policy is to establish the rules, expectations, and requirements for employees working from a location other than a NovaTech Solutions corporate office. This policy ensures that remote work arrangements maintain operational efficiency, comply with data security standards, and support employee well-being.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time NovaTech Solutions employees who request or are assigned to work remotely, either on a hybrid schedule or as fully remote employees. This policy does not apply to third-party contractors unless explicitly stated in their vendor agreements.

## 3. Definitions
*   **Remote Work:** The practice of performing job duties from an approved alternative worksite (e.g., a home office) rather than a central corporate office.
*   **Primary Work Location:** The primary physical address where the employee will conduct their remote work, as documented in their HR file.
*   **Remote Work Agreement (RWA):** The formal, signed document outlining the specific terms, schedule, and expectations of an individual employee's remote work arrangement.
*   **Core Business Hours:** The mandatory block of time during which all employees must be online, reachable, and available for meetings.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Eligibility Period
Employees must successfully complete a 90-day probationary period at NovaTech Solutions before they are eligible to apply for a formal Remote Work Agreement (RWA).

### 4.2 Approval Protocol
All remote work arrangements must be formally requested via the internal HR portal and require documented approval from both the employee's direct manager and the Human Resources Business Partner (HRBP) prior to the commencement of remote work.

### 4.3 Core Working Hours
Remote employees must be online and actively available via corporate communication tools (email, instant messaging) between the Core Business Hours of 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their locally approved time zone.

### 4.4 Workspace Requirements
Remote employees must maintain a dedicated, safe, and distraction-free workspace. The workspace must include a door that can be closed to ensure privacy during confidential business conversations.

### 4.5 Internet Connectivity Minimums
Remote employees must secure and maintain a reliable broadband internet connection with a minimum download speed of 50 Mbps and a minimum upload speed of 10 Mbps. NovaTech Solutions will not reimburse employees for residential internet costs.

### 4.6 Device Usage Restriction
Remote work must be conducted exclusively on NovaTech Solutions-issued hardware (laptops, mobile devices). Processing, downloading, or storing corporate data on personally owned computing devices is strictly prohibited.

### 4.7 Secure Connectivity
Employees working remotely must establish a connection to the corporate network exclusively through the company-approved Virtual Private Network (VPN) client at all times when accessing internal systems, databases, or file shares.

### 4.8 Dependent Care
Remote work is not a substitute for dependent or child care. Employees must have primary care arrangements in place for dependents during their scheduled working hours.

### 4.9 Primary Location Relocation
Employees must submit a formal Location Change Request to HR at least 30 calendar days prior to relocating their Primary Work Location to a different state or country. Relocations are subject to tax and legal review and are not guaranteed to be approved.

### 4.10 Mandatory On-Site Attendance
Fully remote employees are required to attend mandatory in-person quarterly department planning meetings. Managers must provide remote employees with a minimum of 14 days advance written notice for any mandatory on-site events.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Human Resources Department:** Responsible for administering the Remote Work Policy, reviewing RWAs, and managing state/local compliance for remote workers.
*   **Managers:** Responsible for evaluating remote work requests, managing remote team performance, and ensuring equitable treatment of on-site and remote staff.
*   **IT Operations:** Responsible for providing remote access tools, hardware, and technical support.
*   **Employees:** Responsible for maintaining productivity, securing their home workspace, and adhering to all corporate policies while working remotely.

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Medical and Disability Accommodations
The 90-day eligibility period (Requirement 4.1) and mandatory on-site attendance (Requirement 4.10) may be bypassed for employees who require remote work as a formally documented medical accommodation under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), pending HR and Legal approval.

### 6.2 Inclement Weather and Facility Closures
During localized extreme weather events or emergency office closures, employees are permitted to work remotely without an approved RWA for the duration of the facility closure.

### 6.3 Traveling Roles
Field sales and on-site support engineers whose primary roles require constant travel are exempt from the 50 Mbps minimum internet speed requirement (Requirement 4.5) while in transit, but must still comply with all VPN and data security requirements.

## 7. Violations and Enforcement
Failure to comply with the requirements of this policy may result in the immediate revocation of remote work privileges, requiring the employee to return to a NovaTech Solutions corporate office full-time. Severe violations, such as unauthorized international work or security breaches, will result in formal disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

## 8. Policy Review
The Human Resources and Information Security departments will jointly review and update this policy on an annual basis to ensure alignment with employment law, security best practices, and organizational needs.
