# Information Security Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions
**Policy ID:** SEC-POL-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Information Security Department

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to define the baseline security requirements for protecting NovaTech Solutions' information assets from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. This policy establishes a framework to maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of corporate data and IT systems.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all NovaTech Solutions employees, contractors, third-party vendors, and any other individuals or entities that access, process, or store corporate data. It covers all physical and logical systems, networks, endpoints, and cloud environments owned, managed, or leased by NovaTech Solutions.

## 3. Definitions
*   **Information Asset:** Any data, device, or system that holds value to the organization, including source code, customer records, and physical servers.
*   **Data Classification:** The categorization of data based on its sensitivity and the impact to the business should that data be compromised (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted).
*   **EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response):** A cybersecurity technology that continuously monitors endpoint devices to detect and respond to cyber threats.
*   **SOC (Security Operations Center):** The centralized function responsible for monitoring and defending NovaTech Solutions' security posture.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Data Classification Assignment
All newly created information assets must be explicitly assigned a data classification label (Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted) by the data owner within 30 days of the asset's creation.

### 4.2 Encryption at Rest
All digital information assets classified as Confidential or Restricted must be encrypted at rest using the AES-256 encryption standard or a formally approved equivalent algorithm.

### 4.3 Encryption in Transit
All internal and external network transmissions containing Internal, Confidential, or Restricted data must be encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS) version 1.2 or higher.

### 4.4 Endpoint Protection
All company-owned workstations, laptops, and mobile devices must have the standard NovaTech Solutions Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) agent installed, active, and configured with real-time scanning enabled. 

### 4.5 Removable Media Prohibition
The transfer of company data to unauthorized, unencrypted removable media (e.g., USB flash drives, external hard drives) is strictly prohibited. Write access to external USB storage devices must be enforced as "disabled" via centralized endpoint management policies.

### 4.6 Vulnerability Scanning
All externally facing applications and infrastructure (e.g., web servers, public APIs) must be subjected to automated, authenticated vulnerability scanning at a frequency of no less than once every 7 calendar days.

### 4.7 Critical Patch Management
Security patches categorized as "Critical" or "High" by the vendor must be tested and applied to all production servers and network devices within 48 hours of the patch's official release.

### 4.8 Workstation Lock
All interactive user endpoints (workstations, laptops, virtual desktops) must be configured via group policy to automatically activate a password-protected screen lock after exactly 10 minutes of user inactivity.

### 4.9 Incident Reporting Timeline
Employees and contractors must report any suspected or confirmed information security incident to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery, using the dedicated emergency hotline or internal reporting portal.

### 4.10 Third-Party Vendor Assessments
Any third-party vendor or service provider that processes, stores, or transmits Confidential or Restricted data on behalf of NovaTech Solutions must complete and pass a comprehensive Vendor Security Assessment (VSA) annually prior to contract renewal.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Information Security Department:** Responsible for creating, maintaining, and auditing adherence to this policy, as well as managing the SOC and EDR deployments.
*   **Data Owners:** Business unit leaders responsible for determining the appropriate data classification for the information assets generated by their departments.
*   **IT Operations:** Responsible for implementing the technical controls (encryption, patching, endpoint management) mandated by this policy.
*   **Employees and Contractors:** Responsible for complying with security protocols, locking workstations, and promptly reporting suspicious activities.

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Legacy System Compatibility
Legacy systems that are technically incapable of supporting TLS 1.2 (Requirement 4.3) are permitted to use TLS 1.1, provided the system is isolated within a tightly restricted, internal-only network segment and an exception request is documented and approved by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

### 6.2 Extended Patching Windows
Production systems requiring extensive pre-deployment regression testing, or those with zero-downtime SLA constraints, are exempt from the 48-hour critical patch requirement (Requirement 4.7). These systems must be patched within 14 days and require compensating controls (such as customized Web Application Firewall rules) approved by the SOC.

### 6.3 Specialized Research Environments
Dedicated research and development workstations processing extremely large datasets are exempt from the AES-256 encryption at rest requirement (Requirement 4.2) if encryption introduces severe performance degradation, provided these machines are physically secured in the biometric-controlled R&D laboratory.

## 7. Violations and Enforcement
Failure to comply with this Information Security Policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract cancellation. Intentional circumvention of security controls will be treated as a critical violation and may be subject to civil or criminal legal action.

## 8. Policy Review
The Information Security Department will review this policy comprehensively on an annual basis, or more frequently if necessitated by significant changes in the regulatory landscape, organizational structure, or the enterprise threat profile.
