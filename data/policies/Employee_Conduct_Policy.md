# Employee Conduct Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions
**Policy ID:** HR-POL-050
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Human Resources Department

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Employee Conduct Policy is to establish the standards of professional behavior expected from all individuals representing NovaTech Solutions. This policy ensures a respectful, safe, and ethically sound work environment while protecting the company's reputation and assets.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all NovaTech Solutions full-time employees, part-time employees, contractors, interns, and temporary staff operating on company premises, utilizing company systems, or representing the company at external events.

## 3. Definitions
*   **Conflict of Interest:** A situation in which an individual's personal interests or outside activities could inappropriately influence their professional judgment or actions at NovaTech Solutions.
*   **Company Assets:** All physical and digital properties owned or leased by NovaTech Solutions, including laptops, mobile devices, intellectual property, corporate credit cards, and office facilities.
*   **Subordinate:** Any employee who reports directly or indirectly to a manager or supervisor within the organizational hierarchy.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Unplanned Absences
Employees must notify their direct manager via phone call or the approved internal messaging platform at least 2 hours prior to their scheduled shift start time in the event of an unplanned absence or delay exceeding 30 minutes.

### 4.2 Outside Employment Declaration
Employees must submit a formal "Secondary Employment Request" form to the Human Resources portal and receive explicit written approval from both their direct manager and HR before accepting any external employment, consulting, or board positions.

### 4.3 Gift Acceptance Limits
Employees must not accept personal gifts, entertainment, or favors exceeding a total market value of $50 from any single client, vendor, or prospective business partner within a single calendar year. Any gift exceeding this value must be formally declined or immediately surrendered to the Ethics and Compliance Office.

### 4.4 Acceptable Use of Assets
Company-issued computing devices and network resources must not be used to access, download, store, or distribute sexually explicit content, online gambling platforms, or pirated media.

### 4.5 Social Media Disclaimers
Employees who list NovaTech Solutions as their employer on personal, public-facing social media profiles must include the explicit disclaimer: "Opinions are my own and do not reflect the views of NovaTech Solutions" in their profile biography.

### 4.6 Public Workspace Confidentiality
Employees must attach a company-issued privacy screen filter to their laptop monitors whenever viewing or processing data classified as "Internal" or "Confidential" in public spaces, including cafes, airports, and public transit.

### 4.7 Workplace Relationships
Employees holding managerial or supervisory roles are strictly prohibited from initiating or maintaining romantic or sexual relationships with any direct or indirect subordinate. If such a relationship develops, both parties must report it to HR within 5 business days to facilitate an organizational transfer.

### 4.8 Expense Report Submission
All reimbursable business expenses must be submitted through the corporate expense management system within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. An itemized receipt must be attached for every individual expense exceeding $25.

### 4.9 Client Meeting Attire
Employees attending scheduled, in-person meetings with external clients must adhere to a "Business Professional" dress code, which specifically requires wearing a tailored jacket or blazer.

### 4.10 Mandatory Compliance Training
All personnel must complete the annual "Workplace Ethics and Harassment Prevention" training module within 14 calendar days of its assignment date in the learning management system.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Human Resources Department:** Responsible for maintaining this policy, processing outside employment requests, and conducting investigations into alleged conduct violations.
*   **Managers and Supervisors:** Responsible for enforcing these standards within their teams, approving appropriate time-off requests, and modeling compliant behavior.
*   **Employees:** Responsible for familiarizing themselves with this policy, adhering to all stated requirements, and reporting suspected violations to HR or the anonymous ethics hotline.

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Cultural Gift Exception
Ceremonial or cultural gifts exceeding the $50 limit (Requirement 4.3) may be accepted on behalf of the company if refusal would cause significant cultural offense. These gifts require post-acceptance approval from the VP of Human Resources within 7 days and must be displayed in a communal company area rather than retained personally by the employee.

### 6.2 Extended Travel Expenses
Employees deployed on continuous international travel assignments exceeding 30 consecutive days are exempt from the 30-day expense submission rule (Requirement 4.8). These employees are granted a 60-day window from the transaction date to submit their expense reports.

## 7. Violations and Enforcement
Suspected violations of this policy will be investigated by Human Resources. Confirmed violations will result in disciplinary action commensurate with the severity of the offense. Disciplinary measures may include documented warnings, mandatory retraining, suspension without pay, or immediate termination of employment.

## 8. Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed and updated annually by the Human Resources Department, or more frequently if necessitated by changes in employment law or organizational structure.
