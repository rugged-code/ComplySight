# Vendor Management Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions
**Policy ID:** VND-POL-030
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Procurement and Vendor Management Office (PVMO)

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this Vendor Management Policy is to establish a standardized framework for the evaluation, selection, onboarding, ongoing monitoring, and offboarding of third-party vendors. This ensures that NovaTech Solutions mitigates operational, financial, and security risks associated with outsourced products and services.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all NovaTech Solutions employees, contractors, and business units that initiate, manage, or terminate relationships with third-party vendors, suppliers, consultants, or service providers.

## 3. Definitions
*   **Vendor:** Any third-party entity providing goods or services to NovaTech Solutions under a formalized agreement.
*   **High-Risk Vendor:** A vendor that processes, stores, or transmits restricted company data, or provides a service critical to business continuity.
*   **Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA):** A formalized security and compliance evaluation conducted by the Information Security team prior to vendor onboarding.
*   **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** A documented agreement between NovaTech Solutions and the vendor detailing the required level of service and performance metrics.

## 4. Policy Requirements

### 4.1 Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA)
A completed and approved Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA) must be logged in the centralized procurement portal before any contract is signed or purchase order is issued for a new vendor.

### 4.2 Information Security Addendum (ISA)
All contracts with vendors that will process, store, or transmit Confidential or Restricted NovaTech Solutions data must include the standard Information Security Addendum (ISA) signed by the vendor's authorized legal representative.

### 4.3 Financial Threshold Approvals
Any vendor contract with an annualized value exceeding $50,000 USD must be explicitly approved in writing by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and the Head of Procurement.

### 4.4 Annual Compliance Audits
All High-Risk Vendors must submit an independent, third-party security audit report (e.g., SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certification) to the IT Security team annually within 45 days of the vendor's audit completion date.

### 4.5 Subcontractor Disclosure
Vendors must formally disclose all fourth-party subcontractors involved in the delivery of services to NovaTech Solutions. Any addition of a new subcontractor requires 30 days prior written notice to NovaTech Solutions.

### 4.6 SLA Performance Reviews
The designated internal Business Owner for any High-Risk Vendor must conduct and document a formalized SLA performance review meeting with the vendor on a quarterly basis.

### 4.7 Vendor Personnel Background Checks
Vendor personnel who require logical access to NovaTech Solutions' internal network or physical access to restricted facilities must pass a criminal background check prior to being granted access credentials.

### 4.8 Access Revocation
Upon termination of a vendor contract, the internal Business Owner must submit a de-provisioning ticket. IT Operations must revoke all logical and physical access for the vendor's personnel within 24 hours of the ticket submission.

### 4.9 Contract Renewal Notice
Internal Business Owners must initiate the contract renewal or termination evaluation process in the procurement portal at least 60 days prior to the expiration date of the current vendor agreement.

### 4.10 Data Return and Destruction
Within 30 days of contract termination, vendors holding NovaTech Solutions data must provide a cryptographically signed Certificate of Data Destruction confirming that all corporate data has been permanently purged from their systems.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
*   **Procurement and Vendor Management Office (PVMO):** Responsible for maintaining the vendor inventory, facilitating contract negotiations, and enforcing this policy.
*   **Information Security Team:** Responsible for conducting VRAs and evaluating vendor security audit reports.
*   **Internal Business Owners:** Responsible for day-to-day vendor management, tracking SLA performance, and requesting access provisioning/de-provisioning.
*   **Legal Department:** Responsible for reviewing contract terms, liability clauses, and ensuring the ISA is appropriately integrated.

## 6. Exceptions

### 6.1 Emergency Procurement
In the event of a declared critical system outage or life-safety emergency, the requirement for a pre-contract VRA (Requirement 4.1) may be deferred for up to 14 days, provided the emergency procurement is approved by a C-level executive.

### 6.2 Low-Value Subscriptions
Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) subscriptions with an annualized value of less than $1,000 USD that do not process any Confidential or Restricted data are exempt from the formal VRA requirement (Requirement 4.1).

### 6.3 Sole-Source Providers
If a specific vendor is the exclusive provider of a required service (sole-source), the CFO may waive the standard competitive bidding process, but the vendor is still subject to all security and compliance requirements outlined in Section 4.

## 7. Violations and Enforcement
Employees who engage vendors without following the procedures outlined in this policy may be subject to disciplinary action. Vendors that fail to maintain compliance with security requirements or breach SLA thresholds may face financial penalties or immediate contract termination.

## 8. Policy Review
The Procurement and Vendor Management Office (PVMO) must review and update this policy at least annually, or when significant changes occur in the regulatory environment or corporate risk appetite.
